# Glossary

**Elynt Music Tool**: the suite as a whole; one app, personal to its owner (an EDM producer working in Ableton Live), made of three tools.
_Avoid_: "the visualizer" when the whole suite is meant.

**Visualizer**: the tool that turns one of the owner's tracks into reactive visuals, designed with a live preview and delivered as a rendered video.

**Mix & Master Assistant**: the tool that reads the owner's open Ableton Live project and suggests next steps for mixing and mastering it.

**Release Assistant**: the tool that guides the owner through a marketing strategy for a finished track.

**Track**: one finished or in-progress song by the owner. The unit every tool works on.

**Mixdown**: the single stereo audio file of a whole track.

**Stem**: an audio file holding one part of a track (e.g. kick, bass, lead), rendered separately so it can drive visuals on its own.
_Avoid_: "channel", "layer" (a layer is visual).

**Preset**: a saved set of settings for a visualizer, reusable across tracks.

**Layer**: one visual element in a scene, such as a spectrum, particles, or artwork.
_Avoid_: using "layer" for audio parts; those are stems.

**Scene**: a stack of layers composed together into one look.

**Section**: a span of a track with one musical role, such as intro, build, drop, breakdown, or outro.

**Track analysis**: what the Visualizer learns by listening to a track and its stems: tempo, beats, sections, energy, and its emotion and vibe.

**Vision**: the art direction for one track (mood, palette, motifs, the look of each section, and how the visuals react), agreed between the owner and the Visualizer before scenes are built.
_Avoid_: "theme", "style" (a vision is made for one track, not reused as-is).

**Reactivity**: how a layer responds to the audio.

**Live Set**: the owner's Ableton Live project for a track, as saved in its .als file.
_Avoid_: "session", "project file" (when the musical content is meant).

**Live track**: one audio, MIDI, group or return track inside a Live Set.
_Avoid_: "track" alone (a track is the whole song), "channel".

**Bounce**: audio rendered out of a Live Set, either a mixdown or stems, so it can be measured.
_Avoid_: "export" alone (it can also mean a video export).

**Stepping stone**: one concrete, ordered move the Mix & Master Assistant suggests, naming the Live track, the move, where to make it in Live, and why. The owner ticks it off when done.
_Avoid_: "tip", "suggestion", "feedback".

**Mix mode**: the first stage of the Mix & Master Assistant, working on the balance between tracks in a Live Set.

**Master mode**: the second stage of the Mix & Master Assistant, working on the final chain and loudness of the whole track.
_Avoid_: "mastering" for the mode (mastering is the craft; master mode is the tool's stage).

**Reference track**: a released song by another artist that the owner picks for a track, to compare the mix or master against.
_Avoid_: "reference" alone when a visual reference is meant (see the Visualizer).

**Subgenre**: the EDM style the owner tags a track with (e.g. house, dubstep, DnB). It sets the targets the Mix & Master Assistant aims for.
_Avoid_: "genre" (always EDM here), "style".
