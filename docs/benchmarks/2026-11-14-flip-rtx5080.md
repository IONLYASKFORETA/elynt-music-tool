## Verdict

4K60 exports at 3.9× real time, well under the 10× target, so the export stays in Electron. The native wgpu export helper (ADR 0001 fallback) is not needed.

A 10-second run of the same track gave 3.97× at 4K (36.0 dB) and 1.06× at 1080p (34.0 dB). The lower full-track PSNR comes from denser later sections at a fixed bitrate. Most of it is the finish layer's per-frame grain.
