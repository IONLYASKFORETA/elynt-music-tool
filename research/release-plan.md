# Research: what does a strong release plan for an EDM single look like?

Ticket: [#15](https://github.com/IONLYASKFORETA/elynt-music-tool/issues/15) (parent map [#14](https://github.com/IONLYASKFORETA/elynt-music-tool/issues/14), Release Assistant v1).
Researched 2026-11-13 against primary sources: Spotify for Artists support/blog, DistroKid FAQ and help centre, Apple Music for Artists / Apple Music Connect, SoundCloud help centre, TikTok newsroom, YouTube for Artists, Instagram for Creators.
Assumptions from the map: Aden, early-stage independent EDM producer; DistroKid; priorities Spotify, SoundCloud, TikTok, Instagram; $0–100 per release.

**How to read the labels**

- **[Sourced]**: the platform that owns the feature says it on its own page (linked).
- **[Secondary]**: only third-party write-ups say it. Treat it as plausible but unverified.
- **[Folklore]**: common creator advice that no platform states. Fine as a default, but not a rule.
- **[Inference]**: my own reasoning from sourced facts.

---

## Short answer

1. **Release on a Friday.** New music goes out worldwide on Fridays at 00:01 local time (IFPI's Global Release Day, 2015), and Spotify's Release Radar refreshes on Fridays. [Sourced]
2. **Upload to DistroKid about 4 weeks (R−28) before release day. The latest safe upload is R−21.** DistroKid says the release date must be **at least 3 weeks out** for Spotify playlist pitching, and **at least 4 weeks out** to claim Spotify for Artists before a first release. Choosing a custom release date needs the **Musician Plus or Ultimate** plan. [Sourced]
3. **Pitch one song to Spotify editors as soon as it shows under Upcoming in Spotify for Artists.** The hard cutoff is **R−7**. A pitch made by R−7 also puts the song on followers' Release Radar. A song can't be pitched once it is live, and only one pitch can be open at a time. [Sourced]
4. **Use DistroKid's HyperFollow page for Spotify pre-saves.** Spotify's own Countdown Pages are **not available for singles**, and they also need 3,000 monthly active listeners. Ask DistroKid about Apple Music pre-adds. [Sourced]
5. **Make a Spotify Canvas:** a 3–8 s vertical 9:16 MP4 (or JPG), 720–1080 px tall, with no text or vocals. [Sourced]
6. **Short-form video: start teasers about two weeks out (R−14) and keep posting for about 4 weeks after release.** YouTube's own guidance says to post a teaser about two weeks ahead and to keep posting afterwards ("not just one and done"). TikTok's New Release tag runs from R−14 to R+30. Instagram says to "post original content consistently". **None of the platforms gives a posts-per-day figure.** [Sourced for windows; Folklore for daily counts]
7. **SoundCloud:** release on SoundCloud on the same day. The scheduled-release feature requires **Artist Pro**. Turn on the new-track amplification feature, which reaches about 100 matched listeners within 14 days of upload, on the Artist or Artist Pro plan. [Sourced]
8. **Paid tools won't fit a $0–100 budget at Aden's stage.** Marquee needs 5,000 monthly active listeners in the target market. Both Marquee and Showcase need 1,000 streams in the last 28 days. Paying for guaranteed streams breaks Spotify's terms. [Sourced]

## 2. Spotify

### Editorial pitching
From [Spotify: Pitching music and videos to playlist editors](https://support.spotify.com/artists/article/pitching-music-to-playlist-editors/) [Sourced]:
- "Deliver your music at least 7 days before release so our editors have time to listen." Songs **can't be pitched after they go live**.
- **One song pitch at a time.** The next song can be pitched once the pitched song is live.
- Anyone with Admin or Editor access can pitch. "More detail improves the song's chances" (genre, mood, culture, instruments, story). "Pitching doesn't guarantee placement."
- The pitch can be edited up to release day, but editors may not see late edits.
- Music videos can be pitched separately (until 7 days after release). This feature is still rolling out.

There is a conflict between Spotify sources. Spotify's own [Release Guide blog](https://artists.spotify.com/blog/release-guide-preparing-for-release-day) says to pitch "at least two weeks before its release date". The support page gives 7 days as the minimum. DistroKid requires a release date at least 3 weeks out. **Playbook rule: pitch as soon as the song appears in Upcoming (about R−21 to R−28). R−14 is the target and R−7 is the absolute cutoff.**

### Release Radar
From [Spotify: Getting music on Release Radar](https://support.spotify.com/us/artists/article/getting-music-on-release-radar/) [Sourced]:
- "Pitch at least 7 days before release day to pick the song for Release Radar." A pitched song goes to **followers'** Release Radar. Without a pitch, Spotify chooses the song.
- A listener gets at most one song per artist per week. A song can stay for up to **4 weeks** if the listener hasn't played it.
- Remixers don't get Release Radar placement as artists, but remixes as releases are included.

**Why this matters [Inference]:** followers decide Release Radar reach, so following matters more than pre-saves. HyperFollow's pre-save also follows the artist on Spotify (see below).

### Pre-saves: Countdown Pages are not available for singles
From [Spotify: Getting started with Countdown Pages](https://support.spotify.com/us/artists/article/countdown-pages/) [Sourced]:
- Eligible: **new albums and EPs only**. "Singles … do not" qualify. The artist also needs **at least 3,000 monthly active listeners**.
- The page can be created within 72 hours of metadata delivery. Pre-savers get a push notification and the release is added to their library.
- Spotify's figure that "nearly 60% of album pre-savers stream it in the first week" applies to albums.

**Use DistroKid HyperFollow instead** ([What Is HyperFollow?](https://support.distrokid.com/hc/en-us/articles/360013647913-What-Is-HyperFollow), [Sourced]):
- A page is created automatically for every upload and can be promoted "as soon as you finish uploading". It is free. Collecting fan email addresses needs Musician Plus or Ultimate.
- A pre-save **also follows the artist on Spotify** and adds the release to the fan's library. On release day the link automatically adds the other stores, and the URL never changes.

### Canvas
From [Spotify: Canvas guidelines](https://support.spotify.com/artists/article/canvas-guidelines/) [Sourced]: **3–8 s, vertical 9:16, 720–1080 px tall, MP4 or JPG.** Spotify recommends no speech or vocals, no fast cuts or strobing, important content kept away from the edges, no song or artist name, and a complete loop (seamless, hard cut, or rebound). Canvas is added in Spotify for Artists once the track is in Upcoming [Inference from the [Release Guide blog](https://artists.spotify.com/blog/release-guide-preparing-for-release-day), which lists it as a pre-release step].

### Profile and release-day steps (Spotify Release Guide)
[Release Guide: Preparing for Release Day](https://artists.spotify.com/blog/release-guide-preparing-for-release-day) [Sourced]: refresh the profile image and bio, set the **Artist Pick**, add the Canvas, and share **Promo Cards** and Canvas/Clips links after release. Watch real-time streams for the **first 7 days**, check the Audience page after about one week, and check "Release engagement" (the share of followers who streamed) at **28 days**.

### Paid Spotify tools (Marquee and Showcase)
From [Spotify: Getting started with Marquee and Showcase](https://support.spotify.com/us/artists/article/getting-started-with-campaign-tools/) [Sourced]:
- **Marquee:** can only start within **21 days of release**. Needs **5,000 monthly active listeners** in the target market and 1,000 streams in the last 28 days. Runs for 10 days.
- **Showcase:** any release age. Needs 1,000 streams in the last 28 days. Runs for 14 days.
- The page gives no minimum spend.
**[Inference]:** an early-stage artist on $0–100 probably doesn't qualify. The playbook should mark these steps "if eligible" and should not schedule them.

### What to avoid
[Spotify: Artificial streaming and paid services that guarantee streams](https://support.spotify.com/us/artists/article/third-party-services-that-guarantee-streams/) [Sourced]: services that "advertise streams in return for payment violate our terms". Spotify may withhold royalties, remove the track from playlists, or remove the track altogether, and distributors may suspend the account. Leave paid "guaranteed placement" services out of the playbook.

## 3. Apple Music

- **Pre-adds:** fans add the release before launch and are notified when it's out. Independent artists set this up through their distributor ("Work with your label or distributor to add this feature"). Apple says releases with pre-adds saw higher release-week listening. Source: [Apple Music for Artists: Apple Music pre-adds](https://artists.apple.com/support/1118-apple-music-pre-adds). [Sourced]
- **Editorial pitch:** Apple's pitch tool is in **Apple Music Connect** and needs an **Admin or Marketing Manager** role on that account. Pitch "at least 10 days in advance of release for full consideration" and at least 7 days for late adds. Source: [Apple Music Connect: Apple Music Pitch](https://musicconnect.apple.com/support/5575-apple-music-pitch). [Sourced]
- **[Inference]:** Apple Music Connect is a label/distributor-level account, so a DistroKid artist probably can't pitch Apple directly. The playbook should say "no direct Apple pitch". Uploading by R−21 already covers the 10-day window if DistroKid pitches on the artist's behalf. Whether DistroKid pitches to Apple at all is **unverified**.
- **Apple Music for Artists** has a "Promote" tool that generates social assets once the release is live ([Generate assets in Promote](https://artists.apple.com/support/4774-promote-content-milestones-custom-assets)). [Sourced (title and scope only)]

## 4. SoundCloud

- **Scheduled release** is **Artist Pro only**. The track must be uploaded as private (albums and playlists can't be scheduled). It goes live into followers' feeds. Source: [SoundCloud: Schedule a Release Date](https://help.soundcloud.com/hc/en-us/articles/360055603414). [Sourced]
- **New-track amplification (the "First Fans" page):** SoundCloud recommends a new track to about 100 listeners whose taste matches it. Requirements: public, music, longer than 1 minute and under 10 minutes, uploaded or made public within the last **14 days**, not Go+ only. Available on Artist and Artist Pro. Turn it on from the track page. Source: [SoundCloud: Amplify tracks to reach 100+ listeners](https://help.soundcloud.com/hc/en-us/articles/16680415453979-First-Fans). [Sourced]
- **Playbook rule [Inference]:** upload the track to SoundCloud as private ahead of time. Make it public at R−0 (scheduled if on Artist Pro, by hand otherwise) and turn on amplification the same day. A free account means a manual "go public" step at midnight or on the morning of release day.

## 5. Short-form video: TikTok, Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts

### TikTok
- **Artist Account:** open to any artist with released music. The **Artist Tag needs 4 songs on TikTok**. The **New Release** feature "highlights a new track from 14 days before release through 30 days after". Source: [TikTok Newsroom: Introducing the Artist Account](https://newsroom.tiktok.com/en-us/introducing-the-artist-account-to-boost-discoverability-on-tiktok-us). [Sourced]
- **DistroKid fast track (July 2024):** DistroKid users can create a TikTok Artist Account from the DistroKid dashboard, in hours rather than weeks. Source: [TikTok Newsroom](https://newsroom.tiktok.com/tiktok-to-fast-track-artist-account-creation-for-distrokid-members-us?lang=en). [Sourced]
- **TikTok for Artists (June 2025):** gives analytics, plus a **Pre-Release** tool where fans pre-save to their library. It is "available on Spotify and Apple Music only" and requires a certified Artist Account in supported countries (including the US and UK). Source: [TikTok Newsroom: TikTok for Artists launches](https://newsroom.tiktok.com/tiktok-for-artists-launches?lang=en). [Sourced] Whether DistroKid delivers early enough for this to work is **unverified**. TikTok's earlier pre-release clips feature (2022) was only on TikTok's own distributor, SoundOn ([Music Week](https://musicweek.com/digital/read/tiktok-rolls-out-pre-release-feature-for-music-distribution-platform-sound-on/086274)). [Secondary]
- **Posting frequency:** TikTok publishes no number. The usual "1–3 posts a day" is **[Folklore]**.

### Instagram Reels
- "Post original content consistently". US creators can now post reels up to **3 minutes** that are still eligible for recommendation. **Trial reels** are shown to non-followers first and can then be shared with followers. Source: [Instagram for Creators: Finding success on reels in 2025](https://creators.instagram.com/blog/the-latest-with-instagram) (21 Jan 2025). [Sourced]
- Instagram's in-app Best Practices hub lists "sharing reels with watermarks" (for example, reposted TikToks) and "taking credit for content that isn't yours" as things that limit growth. Source: [PetaPixel on the hub launch](https://petapixel.com/2024/10/02/instagram-launches-best-practices-to-help-creators-improve-their-photos-and-reels); see also [Instagram Help: About original content](https://help.instagram.com/1800814370401535/). [Secondary report of first-party in-app guidance]
- **Frequency:** Adam Mosseri (Head of Instagram, 6 Dec 2024) gave no single number. In general, posting more brings more reach, but a sustainable schedule matters more. Source: [his reel](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DDPU_hfSgnZ/) via [RouteNote](https://routenote.com/blog/how-often-you-should-post-on-instagram/). [Secondary report of a first-party statement]
- **[Inference]:** export clean, watermark-free files and upload them to each platform separately. Don't repost TikTok downloads to Instagram.

### YouTube Shorts (secondary priority, but YouTube gives the most specific official timing)
- **Pre-release:** post a teaser or trailer "about two weeks before your release". Use several teaser types (memes/POVs, behind-the-scenes, lip-sync, performance). Use **the same audio snippet across all pre-release Shorts** so they group together, or a Preview if one is available. Post Community countdowns. Add a pre-save link to the channel. Source: [YouTube for Artists: Pre-release strategy](https://artists.youtube/resources/pre-release-strategy/). [Sourced]
- **Release day:** post a Short that day, "always select the official audio from the Shorts audio picker", and Premiere the video on the same day as the song. Same-day Shorts don't hurt recommendations. **After release:** "Upload consistency is key on Shorts, it's not just one and done", and spread other visuals out over time. Source: [YouTube for Artists: Release-day strategy](https://artists.youtube/resources/release-day-strategy/). [Sourced]
- **Frequency:** "Try posting weekly and then re-adjust". Artists who posted weekly got more than 60% of new subscribers from Shorts (Jan 2024 data). Source: [YouTube for Artists: Shorts for artists](https://artists.youtube/resources/shorts-for-artists/). [Sourced]
- Getting the track into the Shorts and TikTok audio libraries depends on DistroKid delivering to those platforms. The opt-in details are **unverified** here.

## 7. Well sourced vs folklore

**Well sourced (stated by the platform that owns the feature)**
- Friday 00:01 global release day. DistroKid's 3-week pitch and 4-week claim rules. DistroKid's 2–5 days to go live. Custom release date needs Musician Plus or Ultimate.
- Spotify: pitch at least 7 days ahead, one song at a time, no pitching after release, Release Radar tied to the pitch. Countdown Pages are albums and EPs only with 3,000 monthly active listeners. Canvas specs. Marquee and Showcase eligibility. Penalties for paid streams.
- Apple: pre-adds go through the distributor. The Connect pitch needs at least 10 days and at least 7 for late adds.
- SoundCloud: scheduling is Artist Pro only. The 14-day amplification window and its criteria.
- TikTok: New Release window from R−14 to R+30. Artist Tag needs 4 songs. Pre-Release saves go only to Spotify and Apple.
- YouTube: teaser about 2 weeks before, official audio picker, same-day Premiere, weekly Shorts as the baseline.
- Instagram: originality and consistency, 3-minute reels are recommendable, trial reels exist, and watermarks limit growth.

**Folklore or unverified (fine as defaults, but don't present them as rules)**
- "Post 1–3 TikToks or Reels a day." No platform gives a number. Mosseri explicitly avoids one.
- "The first 24–48 hours or the first week decide whether the algorithm picks the song up." Not stated by Spotify, TikTok or Instagram. Spotify only frames days 7 and 28 as checkpoints for looking at stats.
- "Pre-save counts make editors notice you." Spotify gives no such signal for singles. HyperFollow's real benefit is the Spotify **follow**, which feeds Release Radar [Inference].
- "Upload 4–6 weeks ahead or editors won't consider you." Spotify's own minimum is 7 days and DistroKid's is 3 weeks. 4–6 weeks is a cautious buffer (SoundCloud's distribution advice), not a rule.
- "Pitch exactly 4 weeks out for the best odds." Not stated anywhere. Spotify's blog says "at least two weeks".
- "Releasing on a non-Friday avoids competition." Untested. Release Radar's Friday refresh is the sourced reason to stay on Friday.
- Whether DistroKid pitches to Apple, and whether its deliveries enable TikTok Pre-Release: **unverified**. DistroKid help pages couldn't be fetched.

## 8. Implications for the Release Assistant (#14)

- Anchor every step to **R = a Friday**. Refuse or warn on a non-Friday date [Inference].
- Model these hard gates with sources: **R−21** DistroKid upload (pitching), **R−7** Spotify pitch and Release Radar, **R+14** SoundCloud amplification, **R+21** Marquee.
- Prerequisites to check once: DistroKid plan tier, Spotify for Artists claimed, TikTok Artist Account, SoundCloud plan (Artist Pro is needed for scheduling).
- Mark Countdown Pages, Marquee and Showcase as eligibility-gated, not default steps. Use HyperFollow for pre-saves.
- Keep cadence numbers as editable defaults labelled "folklore". Keep the windows (R−14 → R+28/30) as the sourced structure.
